# 🎮 Universal Word Guessing Game

Welcome to the **Universal Word Guessing Game**! Your mission is to discover the hidden secret word through strategic guessing and clever deduction.

## 🎯 How to Play

### **Your Goal**
Guess the secret word by making educated guesses based on the feedback you receive.

### **Game Mechanics**
- **Make a guess**: Type any valid English word
- **Get feedback**: Each guess receives:
  - 🏅 **Rank**: How close your word is (lower numbers = closer to the secret)
  - 💡 **Clue**: A hint explaining the connection to the secret word
- **Track progress**: Watch your leaderboard to see your best guesses
- **Use hints**: Type `hint` when you need help (10 hints available)

### **Feedback System**
- **Rank #1-50**: 🔥 You're very close!
- **Rank #51-200**: 🎯 Getting warmer!
- **Rank #201-1000**: 🎲 On the right track
- **Rank #1000+**: 🧭 Keep exploring

### **Commands**
- `[word]` - Make a guess
- `hint` - Get a strategic hint (10 available)
- `quit` - Exit the game

### **Scoring**
- Fewer guesses = better score
- Using fewer hints = bonus points
- Finding the word = victory! 🏆

## 🚀 Ready to Start?

The secret word is waiting to be discovered. It could be anything - an object, concept, place, or idea. Use your intuition, the clues, and strategic thinking to narrow down the possibilities.

**Good luck, word detective!** 🕵️‍♂️
